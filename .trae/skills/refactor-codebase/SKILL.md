# Refactor Codebase

Deliver safe, staged refactors with clear benefits.

## What to Do
- Identify hotspots and refactor targets.
- Propose a staged plan with minimal risk.
- Apply consistent patterns and remove duplication.

## Output Format
- Refactor goals and scope
- Step-by-step plan
- Expected impact and risks

## Example Prompts
- “Refactor this module for clarity.”
- “Reduce duplication across these services.”
