## What's Included

1. **Market Movers**: Biggest price changes in 24h
2. **Arbitrage Opportunities**: Current >2% spreads
3. **Whale Activity**: Smart money movements
4. **News Impact**: Market-moving headlines
5. **Closing Soon**: Markets ending within 48h
6. **Recommendations**: Top 3 opportunities
