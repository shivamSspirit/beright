## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Personalize the first line (reference their work)
- Lead with value/innovation, not asks
- Keep initial DM under 200 words
- Include Loom link (don't force them to read)
- Ask for guidance, not money (initially)
- Follow up once after 5-7 days

**Don't:**
- Sound desperate ("please help", "we really need")
- Make unrealistic claims ("guaranteed returns")
- Send identical messages (they compare notes)
- Ask for money in first message
- Write walls of text
- Spam multiple people at same company
