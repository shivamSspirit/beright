# What is BeRight?

> The Bloomberg Terminal for prediction markets

**BeRight** is a prediction market intelligence platform that aggregates data from multiple platforms, provides AI-powered analysis, and helps you find profitable opportunities in the prediction market ecosystem.

## The Problem

Prediction markets are fragmented. To find the best opportunities, you need to:

- Check multiple platforms (Polymarket, Kalshi, Manifold, etc.)
- Manually compare odds across sites
- Track whale movements without tools
- Miss arbitrage opportunities because you can't monitor everything
- Lack sophisticated analysis tools that exist in traditional finance

## The Solution

BeRight brings everything together in one place:

### Single Source of Truth
All major prediction markets in one interface. Compare odds instantly.

### AI-Powered Analysis
Natural language queries. Ask anything about markets and get intelligent answers.

### Automated Opportunity Detection
Real-time arbitrage alerts, whale tracking, and momentum signals.

## Who is it for?

- **Active Traders** - Find mispriced markets, arbitrage opportunities, and optimal entry points
- **Forecasters** - Track your accuracy, build reputation, and compete on the leaderboard
- **Researchers** - Access aggregated data, historical odds, and market intelligence via API
- **Institutions** - Professional-grade tools, bulk data access, and custom integrations
