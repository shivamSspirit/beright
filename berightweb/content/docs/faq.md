# Frequently Asked Questions

## Getting Started

### What is BeRight?
BeRight is a prediction market intelligence platform that aggregates data from multiple platforms (Polymarket, Kalshi, Manifold, Metaculus, Limitless), provides AI-powered analysis, and helps you find profitable opportunities.

### How do I get started?
The easiest way is through our Telegram bot. Just open [@berightbot](https://t.me/berightai) on Telegram and start asking questions. No signup required.

### Is BeRight free to use?
Yes! The Telegram bot and basic features are completely free. Premium features will be available in future updates.

### Do I need to connect a wallet?
Not for the Telegram bot. Future web app features may require wallet connection for trading and portfolio tracking.

## Trading & Markets

### Which prediction markets does BeRight support?
We currently aggregate data from:
- **Polymarket** (Crypto, Polygon)
- **Kalshi** (Regulated, USD)
- **Manifold** (Play Money)
- **Metaculus** (Forecasting)
- **Limitless** (Crypto, Base)

### What is arbitrage detection?
Arbitrage occurs when the same market has different prices on different platforms. BeRight automatically scans for these opportunities and alerts you when profitable spreads appear.

### How often is data updated?
Market data is updated in real-time. Arbitrage scans run continuously to catch opportunities as they appear.

- `/research <topic>` - Deep analysis on any topic

Yes! You don't need to memorize commands. Just ask questions like:

### How do I get alerts?
Use the `/subscribe` command to subscribe to specific markets or topics. You'll receive notifications when odds change significantly.

## Scoring & Calibration

### What is a Brier Score?
The Brier Score measures forecasting accuracy. Lower is better. A score of 0 means perfect predictions, while 0.25 is equivalent to random guessing.

### How is my forecaster reputation calculated?
Your reputation is based on:
- Prediction accuracy (Brier Score)
- Number of predictions made
- Consistency over time
- Performance on difficult markets

### Can I see other forecasters' track records?
Forecaster profiles and leaderboards are coming in Phase 2. You'll be able to see accuracy scores, prediction history, and rankings.

## Technical

### Is BeRight open source?
Parts of the platform will be open-sourced. The core intelligence engine and smart contracts will be available for community review.

### Do you have an API?
API access is coming in Phase 2. It will allow developers to integrate BeRight intelligence into their own applications.

### What blockchain does BeRight use?
BeRight Protocol is built on Solana for fast, low-cost transactions. Our on-chain programs record forecasts, calibration, and reputation-linked scoring.

### How does the AI analysis work?
We use large language models combined with superforecaster methodology to analyze markets. The AI considers base rates, reference classes, key factors, and historical patterns to generate probability estimates.
