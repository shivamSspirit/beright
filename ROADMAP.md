# BeRight: 18-Month Strategic Roadmap
**Vision:** Conversational AI prediction market infrastructure with decentralized forecaster network

---

## **CURRENT STATE (Month 0 - March 2026)**

### ✅ **What You Have:**
- Multi-platform aggregation (5 platforms)
- Multi-agent AI system (OpenClaw architecture)
- Conviction Pools (Beta - on Solana)
- Web dashboard (Next.js)
- Telegram bot (50+ commands)
- On-chain prediction commits
- Skills system (arbitrage, research, whale tracking)

### 🔴 **What's Missing:**
- Mobile apps
- Trade execution (disabled)
- Full conversational AI interface
- Decentralized forecaster network at scale
- Futures infrastructure
- Multi-primitive support

---

## **PHASE 1: Foundation & Mobile (Q2 2026 - Months 1-3)**

### **Month 1-2: Conversational AI Core**
- **Build chat-first interface** - replace screens with conversational flows
  - Natural language prediction flow: "Bet $50 on Trump winning" → executes
  - Portfolio management via chat: "Show my positions" / "Exit BTC market"
  - Copy trading via chat: "Copy @forecaster123's strategy"

- **Unified AI agent orchestrator** - single conversation handles:
  - Market discovery
  - Research & analysis
  - Position sizing
  - Execution
  - Portfolio tracking

### **Month 2-3: Mobile Apps (MVP)**
- **iOS & Android native apps** (React Native or Flutter)
  - Chat-first UI (no complex screens)
  - Solana wallet integration (Phantom, Solflare)
  - Push notifications (arbitrage alerts, position updates)
  - Swipe-to-predict interface (TikTok-style for markets)

- **Solana Saga integration**
  - Native Seed Vault support
  - dApp Store distribution
  - Saga-specific features (NFC, secure element)

## **PHASE 2: Execution & Network Effects (Q3 2026 - Months 4-6)**

### **Month 4: Trade Execution Engine**
- **Enable real trading** across platforms:
  - Polymarket execution (via CLOB)
  - Kalshi execution (via API)
  - Jupiter integration (token swaps)
  - Position management (entry/exit automation)

- **Risk management layer:**
  - Position sizing algorithms
  - Stop-loss/take-profit automation
  - Portfolio rebalancing

### **Month 5-6: Decentralized Forecaster Network V1**
- **Forecaster reputation system:**
  - On-chain track record (Brier scores)
  - Multi-platform aggregation (Polymarket + Metaculus + BeRight)
  - Verifiable credentials (soulbound NFTs)

- **Delegation marketplace:**
  - Discover top forecasters by performance
  - One-click delegation to Conviction Pools
  - Leaderboards & discovery feeds

- **Copy trading infrastructure:**
  - Subscribe to forecaster strategies
  - Auto-replicate positions (configurable size)
  - Performance attribution

## **PHASE 3: Signal Layer Infrastructure (Q4 2026 - Months 7-9)**

### **Month 7-8: BeRight Signal API**

  - RESTful & WebSocket feeds
  - Real-time arbitrage signals
  - AI research reports
  - Whale wallet activity
  - Market trends & sentiment

- **API tiers:**
  - Free tier (rate-limited)
  - Pro tier (higher limits, priority)

### **Month 8-9: Multi-Primitive Expansion**
- **Add new prediction primitives:**

  - **Categorical markets** (multi-outcome: "Who wins election?")

## **PHASE 4: Futures & Derivatives (Q1 2027 - Months 10-12)**

### **Month 10-11: Prediction Market Futures**
- **Futures contracts on prediction markets:**
  - Long/short exposure without holding positions
  - Leverage (2x-5x) on prediction outcomes
  - Hedging for forecasters (protect downside)

- **Smart contract infrastructure:**
  - Solana perpetual futures program
  - Collateralized positions (SOL/USDC)
  - Liquidation engine
  - Oracle integration (Pyth, Switchboard)

### **Month 12: Options on Predictions**
- **Call/put options** on binary outcomes:
  - "Right to buy YES shares at 60% before Dec 31"
  - Premium-based risk management
  - Volatility trading strategies

BeRight will become the **Bloomberg Terminal for prediction markets** - the infrastructure layer that everyone builds on top of. By combining conversational AI, decentralized reputation, and multi-primitive support, we're creating the signal layer that makes prediction markets accessible, trustworthy, and profitable for everyone from retail traders to hedge funds.

**Target by Month 18:**
- The go-to infrastructure for prediction market signals
- 10,000+ verified forecasters with on-chain reputation
- $100M+ in managed capital through Conviction Pools
- 25+ platform integrations across multiple chains
- Enterprise-grade API serving millions of requests daily
- Autonomous agent marketplace with user-created strategies
- DAO-governed protocol with community ownership

This is not just a prediction market aggregator - it's the foundation for a new financial primitive where cognitive skill becomes tradeable, verifiable, and scalable.
